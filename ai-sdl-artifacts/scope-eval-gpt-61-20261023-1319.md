2. Adopt Sunday–Saturday weeks to meet this rubric; define timezone/date attribution, zero-activity days, the selected metric, aggregation rules, and concrete session-to-cell expected values. Explicitly address browser refresh persistence.
3. Specify audio restrictions, API-unavailability fallbacks, and delayed background notification behavior separately from elapsed-time correctness. Add an inactive-tab completion test.
4. Label requested versus proposed features; document impacts/blocking status for every unresolved decision; distinguish product and engineering choices; identify two dependencies; and define release verification in the supported environment.
